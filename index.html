pCom – Provider Communications

🔒 Secure, HIPAA/PIPEDA compliant, context-aware provider messaging for modern clinical workflows

🌟 Proudly featured in the Harvard SMART Health IT Gallery, showcasing our commitment to interoperable, secure, and clinically integrated SMART on FHIR innovation.

Try it!

SMART EMR Integration



  • Streamline clinical communication with secure, context-aware messaging and FHIR-native workflows.
  • SMART-on-FHIR launch from the EHR with patient, encounter, and provider context preloaded.
  • Advanced search for patients, providers, encounters, and clinical documentation.

🔍 Patient and Provider Registry Integration



  • Integrate with existing client and provider registries to provide accurate identification and validation of resources.
  • Browse and attach encounter information associated with each patient to determine suitability of recipients.
  • Send communications a patient's circle of via CareTeams, Organizations Attachment Registries, and Communication resources.
  • Search CarePlans of each CareTeam associated with a patient.

🔍 Browse a Patient's Documents



  • Find, preview, and attach a patient's Document References, Diagnostic Reports, Observations, Care Plans, Conditions, Media resources - and more - to enrich clinical communication.
  • Search messages by multiple criteria, or define your own search index.

👁️ View Referenced Resources



  • View structured details of any FHIR resource such as Patients, Providers, Encounters, Organizations, or CarePlans.
